[PANEL_L] SUBMISSION PROTOCOL
How It Works — Sequential Workflow
[STEP_01] IDENTITY REGISTRATION

Register your author identity in the right-side manifest. Supply your full legal name, active email, institutional or independent affiliation, and ORCID if available. All metadata fields are required for provenance tracking.

RULE: Pseudonyms accepted only with verified email binding.
[STEP_02] STREAM CLASSIFICATION

Select the publication stream that matches your manuscript category from the editor pane dropdown. Each stream maps to a distinct editorial pipeline with specialized reviewers.

RULE: One stream per submission. Cross-domain papers use INTERDISCIPLINARY.
[STEP_03] MANUSCRIPT COMPOSITION

Compose your headline and body content in the center editor workspace. The body field accepts raw markdown. Use standard formatting: headers (##), bold (**), code blocks (```), and inline references.

RULE: Minimum 500 characters. No binary or encoded payloads.
[STEP_04] LICENSING ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

Review and acknowledge all licensing terms displayed in the right column manifest. Your submission constitutes acceptance of the distribution model, originality provenance requirements, and usage rights as specified.

RULE: Submission implies full acceptance. No partial opt-outs.
[STEP_05] PAYLOAD TRANSMISSION

Execute the COMMIT action at the bottom of the editor pane to transmit your manuscript payload to the editorial review queue. You will receive a confirmation hash and tracking reference via the registered email.

RULE: Submissions are final. Retractions require editorial review.
