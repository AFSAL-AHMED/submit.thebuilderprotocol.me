OFFICIAL SUBMISSION GUIDELINES

Version 1.0 │ Published November 2026 │ The Builder Protocol Editorial Desk

1. PURPOSE & EDITORIAL IDENTITY

Welcome to TBP Submit, the official editorial contribution portal of The Builder Protocol. We welcome original ideas, thoughtful perspectives, technical explorations, research insights, and stories from people interested in technology, engineering, entrepreneurship, research, and innovation.

Submitting an article means agreeing to follow the guidelines below. We evaluate work on its intrinsic merit—whether written by a student embarking on their first technical journey or a veteran principal engineer.

2. WHO CAN CONTRIBUTE?

  • We actively welcome students, researchers, engineers, developers, founders, system architects, and independent domain thinkers.
  • Contributors from all countries, institutions, and professional backgrounds are welcome. Prior publication experience is never required.
  • Articles must be submitted by their actual author or an authorised representative.
  • All listed co-authors must have reviewed and explicitly approved the submission.
  • Contributors under 18 must have appropriate parent or guardian consent prior to public distribution.

3. ACCEPTED ARTICLE TYPES & FORMATS

Format Target Scope Recommended Word Count
Feature Article In-depth architectural or conceptual exploration of a significant topic. 1,500 – 2,500 words
Technical Explainer Demystifies a complex algorithm, protocol, or system architecture. 1,000 – 2,000 words
Research Insight Breaks down an academic paper, experiment, or empirical finding for builders. 1,000 – 1,800 words
Opinion Essay A clearly argued, substantiated personal perspective on technology or society. 800 – 1,500 words
Case Study Detailed examination of an applied project, infrastructure migration, or failure analysis. 1,200 – 2,200 words
Product Teardown Technical investigation of how a software or hardware product works under the hood. 1,200 – 2,000 words
Founder Story First-hand startup mechanics, distribution hurdles, and hard-earned lessons. 1,000 – 2,000 words
Project Showcase Introduction of an open-source project, its code architecture, and lessons learned. 800 – 1,600 words
EDITORIAL NOTE: We distinguish research-based magazine articles from formal peer-reviewed academic papers. An article published in TBP is an insightful publication piece, not an ISSN peer-reviewed academic certification, and TBP never falsely represents it as such.

4. ORIGINALITY & INTEGRITY

  • Your submission must be your original work or work you possess explicit authority to license.
  • All borrowed ideas, code quotations, data, and third-party diagrams must be cited clearly with attribution.
  • Plagiarism, fabricated citations, and misleading empirical assertions are prohibited.
  • Submissions must disclose prior publication on personal blogs or preprints.
  • Recommended standard length: 800–2,500 words. (Exceptions permitted when justified by depth).

5. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE USAGE POLICY

Generative AI tools may be utilized as supplementary writing aids, research synthesizers, or proofreading engines under strict conditions:

  • Material use of AI in drafting or synthesizing must be declared in Stage 4 of the submission matrix.
  • Every factual claim, citation, and equation generated or assisted by AI must be verified by the human author.
  • AI tools cannot be credited as authors or co-authors. Human authors shoulder full legal and factual liability.
  • Fabrication of research findings or interviews using generative AI results in an immediate ban.

EDITORIAL POLICIES & AUTHOR RIGHTS

Governing All Submissions to submit.thebuilderprotocol.me

POLICY A // COPYRIGHT & INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

The Builder Protocol operates on an Author-Retained Ownership model. Authors maintain full copyright over their work. By submitting, and upon acceptance, authors grant TBP a written, non-exclusive publication licence to display, reproduce, distribute, and archive the work across its digital properties.

Reference: Indian Copyright Act, 1957 (Sections 18 & 19 regarding distinction between Assignment and Licensing) & Berne Convention standards.

POLICY B // EDITORIAL INDEPENDENCE

Editorial determinations are rooted exclusively in rigor, clarity, accuracy, and domain relevance. Payment or commercial sponsorship does not guarantee publication, nor can advertisers dictate conclusions. Editors recuse themselves from submissions where a personal or financial conflict exists.

POLICY C // CORRECTIONS, WITHDRAWALS & RETRACTIONS

In alignment with the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) guidelines, post-publication corrections are handled with transparent annotations rather than silent edits. Significant factual mistakes receive documented errata. Retractions exist to preserve the sanctity of the public archive.

POLICY D // PRIVACY & DATA PROTECTION

Author contact information (email, phone, address) is collected strictly for editorial correspondence, provenance tracking, and attribution. Data is never sold or shared with commercial marketers, adhering to the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act standards.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Contributor Inquiries
Can anyone submit an article to The Builder Protocol?
Yes. TBP welcomes submissions from students, engineers, independent researchers, builders, and founders. We judge every piece on its intrinsic clarity and insight, not credentials.
Is there a submission fee or processing charge?
No. There is zero submission fee and zero article processing charge ($0.00). TBP is dedicated to open access knowledge.
Does submitting guarantee that my article will be published?
No. Every submission undergoes evaluation across our 7-stage editorial review pipeline. Acceptance is based on quality, depth, and relevance to the builder ecosystem.
Will The Builder Protocol own my copyright?
No. You retain 100% of your copyright ownership. You grant TBP a written, non-exclusive publication licence to distribute your accepted piece.
Can I submit an article written with AI assistance?
Yes, provided you declare material AI usage in Stage 4 of the submission matrix, personally verify all citations and assertions, and assume complete human responsibility for the piece.
How long does the review process usually take?
Initial screening is typically completed within 5 business days. Full editorial review and feedback loops generally conclude within 10–14 days.
Can I submit research papers?
Yes, we accept technical explainers and research breakdowns. However, note that TBP is an editorial technology publication, not a certified academic peer-reviewed journal.
Can I withdraw my submission?
Yes. You can withdraw your submission at any point before publication by contacting editorial@thebuilderprotocol.me with your TX Hash.
